Privacy Policy

MediMind AI is an educational health-information tool. This page explains in plain words what happens to your data. Template notice: have a qualified professional review this text before you launch publicly.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, optional name, and a password handled by Supabase Auth (we never see your password).
  • Assessment inputs: the symptoms, details, photos, voice recordings, transcripts and lab reports you choose to submit, and the results generated from them.
  • Technical: a one-way hash of your IP address for rate limiting (never the address itself). We do not log medical content.

How it is processed

Your inputs are sent from our secure server functions to Groq's API (open models for text and image understanding, and Whisper for speech-to-text) to produce the assessment. Before that, a built-in safety check looks for emergency wording on your device and again on the server. Live voice transcription in some browsers uses your browser vendor's speech service; you can type instead or use AI transcription (Whisper) of a recording. PDF text is extracted in your browser before upload. We do not sell data and use no advertising cookies.

Where it is stored

Assessments are stored in a Supabase database with Row Level Security, so only your account can read your rows. Photos, audio and reports are kept in private storage buckets inside a folder only you can access and are shown through short-lived signed links. All traffic uses HTTPS. Guest trial results are not saved.

Your choices

  • Delete a single assessment (and its files) from History.
  • Use Delete all my data on the same page to remove every assessment, chat, photo, audio file and report.
  • You are asked for consent before your first upload and can decline by not uploading.

Not for emergencies

If you may be having an emergency, call your local emergency number. This service cannot do that for you.
